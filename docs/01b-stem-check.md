# Open PolyForge STEM check

Verdict: the carried screw-extruder machine (PF1) sits within known science once its die is sized to the supply. **The power budget is the controlling check.** On one 120 V, 15 A circuit (1,440 W continuous under the NEC 125 % rule), heating the process air costs **13.8 W per millimetre of air slot**, so a 150 mm laboratory-width die at the same air design would draw about **3,061 W (25.5 A)** and fails both a 15 A and a 20 A circuit. Melting the polymer is minor: 34 to 48 W at 4 g/min against 663 W for the air. Under Amish's standing instruction of 2026-10-23 the die was narrowed to **40 capillaries and two 0.3 x 48 mm air slots** (F1) with a load manager that measures line current and caps heater duty at 12 A (F2). The fixed design draws about **1,331 W (11.1 A)** steady, 1,643 W (13.7 A) if every assumption takes its upper value, which the load manager trims by cooling the air about 64 K. The machine's nameplate sum (2,480 W, 20.7 A) stays above a 15 A cord limit, so how a load-limited machine is marked and accepted goes to Amish with an electrician's view (OPF-DEC-001 D2). The independent review found a second failing detail: an air knife running parallel to the whole 43 mm nose face would need 43 to 47 kPa, beyond the diaphragm pump; a 5 mm lapped land with a 2 mm plenum behind it brings the air side to 23 to 27 kPa (F5). Of 55 rows for PF1, 20 are within known science and 35 plausible, waiting for TRL 4 evidence; none fails. `stem_check: open`. Method: STANDARDS sections 20 and 25.6a; calculations in `docs/04-calcs/stem_check.py` and `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py` (OPF-CAL-001). The machine makes filter media for testing; it makes no respirator claim (OPF-CLM-001).

## Fixes made under the standing instruction (2026-10-23)

Each keeps the working principle (a melt extruded through capillaries and drawn by hot air, collected and corona charged) and is recorded in OPF-DEC-001 and OPF-DDR-003.

- **F1 Die width set by the power budget.** 40 capillaries and two 0.3 x 48 mm slots at 0.10 g/hole/min instead of a laboratory-width die (S1).
- **F2 Load manager.** Measures line current and caps summed heater duty at 12 A; warm-up sequenced (S2, S3, S55).
- **F3 Corona interlocks.** HV enabled only with the door guard-locked, extraction airflow proven and the drum turning; current-limited, fixed negative supply; ozone monitor at the operator (S27, S33).
- **F4 Fume capture and guarding at the extruder.** Branch hood over the extruder outlet and a fixed perforated lid on the hopper (S28, S31).
- **F5 Air knife land and plenum.** A 5 mm lapped land at 0.3 mm with a 2 mm plenum behind it; gaps set with shims at temperature; pump raised to the 200 NL/min, 30 kPa class (S7, S41, S45).
- **F6 Groove size.** 0.27 mm square grooves, which flow as 0.30 mm round holes (S12).
- **F7 Doffing cycle.** Melt pump stopped and die held hot while the sheet is cut off; output reported as batch (S20).
- **F8 Heater low-flow protection.** Pressure switch at the heater inlet, check valve after the pump, pump run-on (S42).
- **F9 Heated melt tube.** 100 W band heater on its own zone (S47).
- **F10 Fire controls.** Insulated heater shell outside the fibre stream, cleanable duct, fly cleaned each shift, extinguisher at the bench (S49).

## Independent review

A separate agent, run as its own process on 2026-11-12 (`claude -p`, Opus model, with only `stem_results.csv`, `stem_results_options.csv`, `stem_check.py`, `sizing.py`, `sizing_results.json`, the design parameters and the source list, not this document's reasoning), tried to refute every "Within known science" verdict and to find missed claims. Of 28 such rows, **14 held** (S4, S5, S9, S11, S12 with a note, S19, S21, S24, S34, S39, X2, X5, X9, X11), **13 were weak** (S1, S2, S7, S10, S16, S20, S29, S31, S37, S40, X1, X7, X12) and **1 was refuted** (X6). Of the plausible rows it judged one a fail (X8) and one wrongly quantified (S3). It listed **27 missed claims** (M1 to M27). I checked each finding with my own numbers before changing anything:

- **S7 and M1 (weak, near fail):** confirmed. The slot is laminar (Re 1,933 by Sutherland viscosity); with the knife parallel to the whole 43 mm nose face the developed-flow loss alone is 22 kPa and the air side 43 to 47 kPa, beyond the pump. Fixed by F5 (S41 now 23 to 27 kPa) and the pump raised to the HP-200 class (30 kPa maximum continuous); the margin is thin, so S7 is now plausible.
- **S2 and M7 (weak):** confirmed. Nameplate sum 2,480 W (20.7 A) with the larger pump and the melt tube heater; S2 is now plausible and the marking goes to Amish (D2). The load manager measures current, not power (S55).
- **S3:** confirmed; the trim is about 64 K at the pessimistic case, not 20 to 30 K, which puts the air near 206 C, below the cited range; recorded as a risk to measure.
- **S1 (weak):** confirmed; the reference case now adds two pumps (3,061 W) and "with margin" is withdrawn.
- **S12 note and M10:** confirmed (a 0.30 mm square groove passes 43 % more than a 0.30 mm hole); grooves are now 0.27 mm (F6) and S14 uses the square-duct formula and the 250 C viscosity (M13).
- **S29 (weak):** confirmed; 450 to 470 C is a thermogravimetric figure; the row now states that oxidation does occur at the set points and is measured.
- **S31 and M21 (weak):** confirmed; the extruder sits outside the enclosure. Hopper lid and branch hood added (F4); S31 now plausible.
- **S37 (weak):** confirmed; depth was the bench value. Now 600 mm from the model's bounding box, after moving the interlock switch inside, slimming the hinges and narrowing the enclosure; the reviewer's point also exposed that the drum motor would hit the back panel at full traverse, so it moved beside the drum on a timing belt (OPF-DDR-002).

- **X6 (refuted):** confirmed: Molina's spinneret is a 15 cm aluminium ring, not a 60 mm cup. PF3 was remodelled on the 150 mm ring (27.5 m/s rim, 10,075 m/s2).
