# 0002: Design for construction

Status: made under STANDARDS section 18 (Amish, 2026-09-30: "fix the design assumptions to match and be physically feasible"). None changes what the machine does, its pitch or its safety case.

| # | The concept had | The constructable design has | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A precision die needing 5 or 6 axis machining for drilled capillaries | A split die: 40 grooves 0.27 mm square cut into the lapped face of the lower half on a 3-axis mill, closed by the upper half; four M6 clamp screws | Micro-drilling 40 holes of 0.3 mm on a nose is a specialist job; a grooved split face is a milling job and can be opened for cleaning |
| 2 | Air knives as separate parts with no stated fixing | Two stainless knives with flanges screwed to the die halves (two M5 each), the land gap set with shims at temperature | The gap must be adjustable and repeatable |
| 3 | Hot parts on a frame | Steel stand blocks with 6 mm glass-mica insulator pads under the die, the melt pump and the barrel | Limits heat loss to the frame and keeps the frame touchable |
| 4 | Modules on a bench | A welded SHS 40 base frame coach-screwed to the bench; five 6 mm mounting plates fixed with M8 bolts into rivet nuts in the rail top walls | Nuts inside a closed section cannot be reached |
| 5 | A drum motor on the shaft end | Motor beside the drum on the carriage, 24 to 40 mm timing belt drive | At full traverse the end motor would hit the back panel (found while checking the envelope) |
| 6 | Interlock switch on the door outside | Switch inside on the post, tongue on the door; hinges slimmed; enclosure 592 mm deep over its panels | Keeps the machine inside the 610 mm bench depth (model envelope 600 mm) |
| 7 | Melt pump drive unstated | NEMA 23 with 10:1 planetary gearbox on a folded bracket, jaw coupling | 34 rpm at high torque |
| 8 | Enclosure panels across the base rails | End panels notched round the rails; melt tube and air line through 30 mm and 18 mm holes | Panels sit flush on the bench |
