# Open PolyForge claims register

What the project may say, what it may say only after laboratory testing, and what it must never say. Method: STANDARDS section 25.5c. A machine that makes filter media is not a respirator and makes no respirator claim.

Table 1. Claims.

| Subject | May claim now (design intent) | Only after laboratory testing | Never claim | Limits to state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The machine (PF1, and PF2 or PF3 if chosen) | "A machine that makes melt-blown polypropylene web"; "designed to run on one 120 V, 15 A circuit with a load manager"; "targets fibres of 1 to 5 um and a basis weight of 25 g/m2" | Measured line current, output, fibre diameter and basis weight | That it is certified, approved or medical-grade, or that it makes respirators or respirator-grade media | Workshop equipment for technically capable operators; concept prototype |
| The web | "Melt-blown polypropylene web"; "corona charged" | "Measured filtration efficiency at 0.3 um" (NaCl, stated flow and face velocity) with "measured pressure drop" and charge decay after ageing; "targets the media efficiency used in" a named respirator class, as a benchmark | Any respirator class name as a property of the media or the machine; 100 % filtration; protects against any disease; prevents infection; sterile | Not a respirator; media properties vary run to run; charge decays with heat and humidity |
| Downstream use | That downstream projects may evaluate the media | Nothing beyond the measured media data | That the media makes a mask or respirator compliant | Each downstream product needs its own certification |

Table 2. Benchmarks cited as targets for a finished respirator only (not passes, not for this machine or media).

| Benchmark | Requirement | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NIOSH 42 CFR Part 84, 84.181 ([text](https://govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2009-title42-vol1/pdf/CFR-2009-title42-vol1-sec84-181.pdf)) | NaCl CMD 0.075 um at 85 L/min after 25 h at 38 C and 85 % RH; the N95 class benchmark needs at least 95 % per filter | Downstream benchmark; the NaCl method is the media test benchmark |
| EN 149 | European filtering half masks | Downstream benchmark |
| GB 2626 | Chinese respiratory protective equipment | Downstream benchmark |

## On-machine wording (proposed, awaiting Amish)

"Open PolyForge CONCEPT PROTOTYPE. Makes melt-blown polypropylene web for testing. Not a respirator; media from this machine is not for masks or respirators unless laboratory tested and the finished product is certified to its own standard. Hot surfaces, high voltage, moving parts: run only with the door shut and the exhaust on. Emergency stop: red buttons."

## Release limitation

No media from a prototype machine is used in a mask or respirator until its efficiency, pressure drop and charge decay are measured by a qualified laboratory and the finished product is certified.
